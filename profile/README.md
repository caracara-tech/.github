# Caracara Tech

**Open scientific instrumentation for everyone.**

Caracara Tech develops open scientific instruments using digital fabrication, accessible electronics, and open-source technologies.

Our goal is to make scientific tools more affordable, repairable, reproducible, and accessible to schools, researchers, makers, and communities around the world.

## OpenFlexure Microscope

Our current platform is based on the [OpenFlexure Microscope](https://openflexure.org/), an open scientific instrument designed for accessible and precise digital microscopy.

The system combines:

- 3D-printed mechanical components
- Modular optical systems
- Open electronics
- Programmable control
- Locally manufacturable components
- Open documentation and designs

## Future Platforms

The OpenFlexure Microscope is the starting point for a broader portfolio of open scientific instruments.

Caracara Tech plans to develop additional scientific instruments and platforms across different areas of research and education, using the same principles of accessibility, local manufacturing, modularity, and openness.

Our aim is to build a growing ecosystem of scientific tools that can be reproduced, adapted, and improved by communities around the world.

## Our Approach

### Accessible

Scientific instruments should not be limited by the cost or availability of conventional laboratory equipment.

We design systems that can be built using widely available components and digital fabrication technologies.

### Sustainable

Local manufacturing can reduce dependence on complex supply chains and make instruments easier to maintain and repair.

Our goal is to make mechanical components locally manufacturable wherever possible.

### Open

Designs, software, electronics, and protocols should be available for others to study, reproduce, modify, and improve.

## Technology

Caracara Tech combines:

## Mission

We believe access to scientific instrumentation should not be constrained by geography, infrastructure, or cost.

Our approach is simple:

> Build locally. Repair indefinitely. Share openly.
